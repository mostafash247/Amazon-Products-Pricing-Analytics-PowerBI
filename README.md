# 📊 Amazon Products & Pricing Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, end-to-end Power BI analytics project analyzing Amazon product performance, pricing dynamics, and customer engagement metrics. Developed as part of the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)** powered by the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) in collaboration with **EYouth**.

---

## 📌 Project Objectives & Key Insights
- **Pricing & Discount Strategies:** Evaluated how discount bands influence overall pricing structures and customer demand.
- **Customer Sentiment & Satisfaction:** Analyzed average ratings and review distributions across key product categories to identify top-performing segments.
- **Interactive Decision-Making:** Provided business-driven metrics to optimize product catalog performance and pricing strategies.
- **Enhanced UX/UI:** Designed an immersive 3D landing cover (Welcome Page) with seamless multi-page navigation.

---

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Data Modeling & Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL)
- **UI/UX Design:** Custom Canvas & 3D Interactive Layouts
